# Data visualization examples

### Five Distinct Debt Trajectories After the Global Financial Crisis

The 2008 global financial crisis marked a turning point in government debt for several major economies. Using data from the OECD, this visualization compares government debt as a percentage of GDP across Japan, Greece, Ireland, Germany, and the United States between 1995 and 2019.

While debt increased substantially in several countries following the crisis, the long-term trajectories differed considerably. Ireland and Germany experienced significant declines from their post-crisis peaks, while Japan and Greece maintained much higher debt burdens.

**Source:** [OECD Data Explorer](https://data-explorer.oecd.org/vis)
